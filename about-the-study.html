The HIMALAYAS Trial = Harmonized Interventions to Maintain health via Appropriate risk factor modification and Lifestyle changes in Pediatric, Adolescent, and Young Adult cancer Survivors

About The Study

Study Description

Cancer-related cardiovascular disease is a major health concern and a leading cause of death in select groups of pediatric, adolescent, and young adult cancer survivors (AYAs). Cardiovascular disease affects ~33% of AYAs. The American Heart Association and American Cancer Society recently endorsed cardiac rehabilitation-based care to improve cardiovascular disease risk and mortality in survivors of adult cancers. Yet, AYAs are not referred to cardiac rehabilitation services as they do not meet typical program eligibility criteria.

Goal

Using a randomized controlled design, this study is designed to evaluate the impact of a cardio-oncology rehabilitation (CORE) program on cardiovascular and psychosocial health in AYAs with mild cancer-related heart dysfunction (called stage B heart failure). Overall, this work may enhance care and healthcare experiences for AYAs in Canada by helping establish a new standard of care for cancer-related cardiovascular disease risk management in AYAs living with stage B heart failure. Moreover, this exportable model of care could potentially reduce the duration, dose, and even the need for long-term pharmaceutical interventions to control cardiovascular disease risk in AYAs, resulting in urgently needed cost savings for patients and the healthcare system.

Study Procedures

All participants will undergo:

  • Screening echocardiogram to confirm eligibility
  • Monthly emails with a link to a brief check-in survey on REDCap to record SAEs/AEs, etc.

Baseline visits will include:

  • Echo
  • ECG
  • Bloodwork
  • Clinic Visit

6-month visit will include:

  • Echo
  • ECG
  • Bloodwork
  • Clinic Visit
  • CPET
  • PROs Questionnaires
  • ASA24 Dietary Survey

12-month visit will include:

  • Echo
  • ECG
  • Bloodwork
  • Clinic Visit
  • CPET
  • PROs Questionnaires
  • ASA24 Dietary Survey

24-month visit will include:

  • Echo
  • ECG
  • Bloodwork
  • Clinic Visit
  • CPET
  • PROs Questionnaires
  • ASA24 Dietary Survey

CORE Group:

  • Exercise Therapy
  • Bike exercise Led by specially trained staff, using CPET results to prescribe and deliver standardized, yet individually tailored, HIIT sessions
  • CVD risk factor management for first 6-months of exercise intervention
  • Behavioral support for the entire 2-year intervention period
  • Will be provided a wrist-worn heart rate and PA monitor

CON Group:

  • Receive standard medical care/follow-up and a copy of cancer exercise guidelines

KT Sub-Study

Are you a Pediatric, Adolescent, and Young Adult Cancer Survivor (PAYA-CS) who either completed, started but did not finish, or declined participation in the HIMALAYAS trial?

Researchers at the University Health Network in Toronto, Ontario are conducting 45 minute phone interviews to understand the following:

For those who completed the study or participated but did not complete the study: People's experiences participating in the study (e.g. what you liked and disliked about the overall intervention and its components; barriers to participating; and what aspects of the intervention helped you achieve your health and fitness goals)

For those who declined the study: People's experiences and impression of the study (e.g. your understanding of the goals of the trial; barriers to participating; and, if and how any aspect of the study can be improved).

If you are interested in having your voice heard, please email Dr. Michelle Nadler, Principal Investigator of Knowledge Translation sub-study, at michelle.nadler@uhn.ca to learn more about the study.

As a token of appreciation, you will receive an Amazon gift card for participating.
