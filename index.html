About The Study

The HIMALAYAS Trial = Harmonized Interventions to Maintain health via Appropriate risk factor modification and Lifestyle changes in Pediatric, Adolescent, and Young Adult cancer Survivors

Cancer-related cardiovascular disease is a major health concern and a leading cause of death in select groups of pediatric, adolescent, and young adult cancer survivors (AYAs). Cardiovascular disease affects ~33% of AYAs.

Using a randomized controlled design, this study is designed to evaluate the impact of a cardio-oncology rehabilitation (CORE) program on cardiovascular and psychosocial health in AYAs with mild cancer-related heart dysfunction (called stage B heart failure).
